# Mass Consume

One Consume command for the whole selection: drafted pawns get a Consume button showing how many of them carry something consumable - `Consume (6/7)` - and picking an item from the list makes every carrier eat or drink one right from their pockets.

## What it does

- Clicking the button drops a list of everything carried, with a per-item carrier count: `Simple meal (6/7)`, `Beer (2/7)`, `Go-juice (1/7)`.
- Shift-click list entries to queue: meal first, then beer, then go-juice - pawns work through the queue in order.
- Anything consumable works: meals, raw food, drugs, alcohol and modded or DLC items - the game's own edibility checks decide, so food restrictions, drug policies and traits are respected.
- Works for a single selected pawn too - a handy "consume something" button covering food as well as drugs. Animals and colony mechs don't get the button (they don't eat from pockets).
- Works with all mods by design - consuming uses the vanilla inventory-tab pipeline; safe to add or remove at any time.
